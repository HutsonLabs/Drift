# Drift 26.10

Drift now runs on iPhone and iPad, and comes with Drift Host, a Mac host for reaching your Mac
from any Drift client.

- Drift for iPhone and iPad: live desktop, keyboard, mouse, trackpad and Pencil input, touch
  modes, an iPhone layout with an on-screen trackpad, clipboard, and a session switcher.
- Drift Host for Mac: screen streaming with H.264 (AVC420 and AVC444) and HEVC, pairing with a
  code or QR, a virtual display sized to the device, clipboard, sound, and a menu-bar app.
- Mac-specific extras between Drift ends: precise scrolling and trackpad gestures, the Mac's
  menus in the iPad menu bar, and quality that adapts to the link.
- GNOME Remote Desktop hosts keep working as before.
- Macs and GNOME computers now look and behave like what they are: a Macs / GNOME filter, one
  "+" that asks which kind you are adding, a Mac's own settings, the GNOME mode or the Mac's
  quality in the title bar, GNOME menus in the iPad menu bar, and a key row for each on iPhone.

## What to Test

Tap "+" and pair your iPhone or iPad with Drift Host on a Mac (Pair a Mac…, then scan the code),
connect, and try typing, the trackpad, scrolling, copy and paste, and switching sessions. Check
that the Mac's card, settings and title bar say Mac, and that the iPhone key row offers ⌘Space
and ⌘Tab. Add a GNOME computer too and try Activities and Show Apps from the iPad menu bar.
